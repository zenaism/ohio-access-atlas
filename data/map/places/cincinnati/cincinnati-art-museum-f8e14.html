Summary

Accessible restroom (First Floor, northwest corner of Great Hall; Ground Floor has the most); adult changing table (adjustable universal changing station, Ground Floor (adult size implied, not stated)); elevator / lift (Elevators A-D; A links floors G-2 near Cincinnati Wing); wheelchair loan (free, no reservation, coat rooms); accessible parking (Main Entrance, Dewitt Entrance (ground floor, back lot), near Art Climb; parking free).
