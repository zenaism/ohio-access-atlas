Summary

Step-free entrance (front entrance at top of sloped driveway (2016 guide)); accessible restroom (throughout Union Terminal); adult changing table (3 tables, up to 400 lb, in the universal restrooms); elevator / lift (wheelchair lift, Get Into Nature gallery); wheelchair loan (rental at box office: wheelchair $7, ECV $29, 18+, ID held, cashless). Sources dated up to 2026-11.

Getting there

  • Arriving: The venue says: front entrance at top of sloped driveway (2016 guide). From the Museum Center bus stop, the place is about 300 feet to the northwest, in a straight line.
  • 4 path sections steeper than a ramp (8.33%) within 500 feet; steepest about 31.3%.
  • 13 mapped stairways within 500 feet.
  • 3 city-mapped curb ramps within 500 feet.
