Summary

Step-free entrance (both pedestrian bridges from the parking lots are ADA accessible); adult changing table (adult-sized changing tables (Access for All maps 2026)); wheelchair loan (wheelchair $10; ECV $30 reserved day before (2016 prices)); getting around inside limited (main paths yes; Jungle Trails rough terrain). Getting there

  • Arriving: The venue says: both pedestrian bridges from the parking lots are ADA accessible. From the Vine St 3444 bus stop, the place is about 600 feet to the northeast, in a straight line. A step-free route from that stop is about 2,308 feet (directions in Details).

Step-free route from the Vine St 3444 bus stop

About 2,308 feet. About 210 feet longer than the shortest way, to avoid 2 stair sections. No street crossings.

  1. Head southeast on the Vine Street sidewalk for 690 feet.
  2. Bear left onto the Erkenbrecher Avenue sidewalk for 430 feet.
  3. Turn left onto a footpath for 0.2 miles.
  4. The route ends at the building's mapped location; no accessible door is mapped, so use the entrance the venue describes above.

Suggested from OpenStreetMap paths and USGS elevation; avoids mapped stairs and steep sections. Not checked on site: sidewalks and curb ramps may be missing from the map.
