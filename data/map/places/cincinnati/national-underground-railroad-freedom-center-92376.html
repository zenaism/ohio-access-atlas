Step-free route from the Rosa Parks & Freedom Way bus stop

About 532 feet. This is also the shortest way. No street crossings.

  1. Head south on the Rosa Parks Street sidewalk for 80 feet.
  2. Turn left onto the East Freedom Way sidewalk for 280 feet.
  3. Turn left onto a footpath for 180 feet.
  4. The route ends at the building's mapped location; no accessible door is mapped, so use the entrance the venue describes above.
