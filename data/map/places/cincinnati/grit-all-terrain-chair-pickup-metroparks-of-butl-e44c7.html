What the sources say

  • Accessibility contact: yes

    Accessibility Coordinator, swingate@yourmetroparks.net

    “by phone at (513) 867-5835, or by postal mail at MetroParks of Butler County, 2051 Timberman Road, Hamilton, Ohio, 45013.”

    Source · 2026-11-11

  • All-terrain wheelchair: yes

    1 adult + 1 child GRIT chair; pick up at Voice of America MetroPark Welcome Center (7850 VOA Park Dr, West Chester Twp), weekdays 8:30-4:30

    “MetroParks of Butler County is proud to announce that two new GRIT wheelchairs are now available for public use.”

    Source · 2026-11-11

  • All-terrain wheelchair: yes

    may be used at any park, not only MetroParks

    “Users are welcome to take the GRIT chairs to any park; they do not need to stay at Voice of America MetroPark or in the MetroParks system.”

    Source · 2026-11-11

  • All-terrain wheelchair: yes

    reservation free; refundable $75 deposit

    “A refundable $75 deposit is required at the time of reservation.”

    Source · 2026-11-11

  • All-terrain wheelchair: yes

    GRIT Spartan: pavement, grass, dirt, primitive trails; seat 20 in, 300 lb limit; user brings helmet

    “-This non-motorized chair is designed to be used on pavement, grass, dirt, and primitive hiking trails”

    Source · 2026-11-11

  • Mobility devices: yes

    wheelchairs allowed anywhere open to pedestrians; OPDMD rules by area

    “shall be permitted in any areas open to pedestrian use.”

    Source · 2026-11-11

  • Service animals: yes

    welcome at all facilities and outdoor spaces

    “Service animals trained to perform a specific task or tasks for a person with disabilities are welcome at all MetroParks facilities and outdoor spaces.”

    Source · 2026-11-11
