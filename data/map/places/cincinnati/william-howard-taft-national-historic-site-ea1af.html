Summary

Step-free entrance (curb ramp to Education Center on Auburn Ave); accessible restroom (Education Center men's and women's); elevator / lift (Taft House, basement, north side); accessible parking (2 front + 2 rear spaces listed on the accessibility page); path surface limited (site paths paved and pebble-covered, gradual incline). Sources dated up to 2024-09.

Step-free route from the Auburn Ave & Mason St bus stop

About 290 feet. This is also the shortest way. No street crossings.

  1. Head south on Auburn Avenue for 90 feet.
  2. Turn right onto the Auburn Avenue sidewalk for 60 feet.
  3. Turn left onto a footpath for 140 feet.
  4. The route ends at the building's mapped location; no accessible door is mapped, so use the entrance the venue describes above.
