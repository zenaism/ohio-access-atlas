Summary

Step-free entrance (Reds: fully accessible ballpark); elevator / lift; accessible parking; wheelchair seating (most accessible seats have electrical outlets; escorts to seats). Sources dated up to 2026-11.

Step-free route from the RTC Exit bus stop

About 1,084 feet. About 290 feet longer than the shortest way, to avoid 1 steep section. 1 street crossing: 1 with traffic signals (0 audible).

  1. Head east on East Transit Center for 120 feet; no sidewalk mapped here.
  2. Cross at the traffic signal, 50 feet (no audible signal mapped).
  3. Turn right onto a pedestrian walkway for 910 feet; caution: 1 short section steeper than a ramp.
  4. The route ends at the building's mapped location; no accessible door is mapped, so use the entrance the venue describes above.
