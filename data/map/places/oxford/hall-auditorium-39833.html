Summary

Notice: Miami Student, Nov 2019: stage stairs unmarked, railing on one side only (low vision). Step-free entrance (for wheelchair users: rear service doors from the south lot; an usher takes you to the elevator); accessible parking (one-way drive south of Hall Auditorium off Campus Ave; lots behind Hall and McGuffey); wheelchair seating (accessible and companion seats chosen when buying assigned-seat tickets). Sources dated up to 2026-11.

Getting there

  • Arriving: A wheelchair-accessible main entrance is mapped on the north side. The venue says: for wheelchair users: rear service doors from the south lot; an usher takes you to the elevator. From the Campus & Walnut SB bus stop, the accessible entrance is about 250 feet to the east, in a straight line. A step-free route from that stop is about 277 feet (directions in Details).

Step-free route from the Campus & Walnut SB bus stop

About 277 feet. This is also the shortest way. 1 street crossing: 1 marked without a signal.

  1. Head east on the South Campus Avenue sidewalk for 10 feet.
  2. Cross at the marked crosswalk, 40 feet (no signal).
  3. Continue onto a footpath for 230 feet.
