Summary

Step-free entrance; accessible parking (off Weeb Ewbank Way, northwest corner behind Millett); wheelchair seating (accessible and companion seats chosen when buying assigned-seat tickets). Sources dated up to 2026-11.

Getting there

  • Arriving: A wheelchair-accessible entrance is mapped on the west side. 4 accessible entrances are mapped in all. The venue says: use the northwest entrance (closest to the elevator); wheelchair seating cannot be reached from the main concourse. From the Sycamore & Tallawanda bus stop, the accessible entrance is about 900 feet to the north, in a straight line. A step-free route from that stop is about 1,136 feet (directions in Details).

Step-free route from the Sycamore & Tallawanda bus stop

About 1,136 feet. This is also the shortest way. 2 street crossings: 2 marked without a signal.

  1. Head north on Tallawanda Road for 80 feet; no sidewalk mapped here.
  2. Cross at the marked crosswalk, 100 feet (no signal).
  3. Turn right onto the East Sycamore Street sidewalk for 40 feet.
  4. Turn left onto a footpath for 920 feet.

Route with safer crossings (for blind or low-vision travelers)

About 1,150 feet. Prefers crossings with traffic signals and avoids unmarked ones. 1 street crossing: 1 marked without a signal.
